# drift recovery

## purpose

When overwhelm or novelty creates an urge to restart everything, compare that urge with the current plan and real evidence before building a parallel system.

## trigger

Overwhelm, abandonment, or escape-driven restart language appears during active work. A calm redesign proposal is ordinary planning and does not trigger this workflow.

## steps

1. Restate the current plan, present position, and next action in three bullets or fewer.
2. Ask what specifically failed: a broken result, missing capability, contradiction, or merely a feeling of messiness.
3. If nothing concrete failed, keep the structure and shrink the next action.
4. If something failed, propose the narrowest repair inside the current structure.
5. A genuine redesign requires a calm owner decision after seeing migration cost and lost-progress risk.

## boundaries

- Do not diagnose the owner or moralize about discipline.
- Do not activate this skill unless the interview or real use shows the pattern.
- The owner's current informed decision still wins.
