# inbox

**Bottom line:** Temporary waiting room for personal material whose owner or destination is not yet clear. It is safe to capture first and organize later.

**When to read this:** Read when adding, reviewing, or routing uncertain personal material.

Use `../../os/skills/inbox-triage.md`. Do not let the inbox become permanent storage, and do not commit ignored raw attachments until privacy and ownership are reviewed.
