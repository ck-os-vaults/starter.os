# your simple setup guide

**Audience:** Owner
**Lifecycle:** Optional setup reading — archive with the `setup/` folder after onboarding.

**Bottom line:** You will have one private folder that teaches an AI agent who you are, where your important context lives, how you prefer to work, and what it must ask before doing. One guided chat handles the setup.

**When to read this:** Read this if a friend or service provider gave you the starter and you want the shortest explanation before beginning.

## this is not a coding project

You are building an **agent context machine**: ordinary readable documents that help Codex or Claude understand your life and work without relying on one giant chat history.

- **Obsidian** gives you a comfortable place to read the documents.
- **Codex or Claude** helps you think and works with the documents.
- **GitHub and GitLab** keep two private, matching online histories.
- **A local backup tool** keeps a third daily copy on a drive you control.

You do not need to learn the technical machinery. You should understand what is private, what action you are approving, and where your recovery information lives.

## begin

1. Open this folder in your agent.
2. Open `setup/FIRST-CHAT.md`.
3. Paste its prompt into the chat.
4. Answer naturally. There are no perfect answers.
5. Complete private sign-in and security steps yourself when the agent pauses.

The agent will not reorganize your life during the interview. It will fill the existing context documents first and add structure only when your real needs clearly require it.

## three rules

1. Put uncertain material in `00_inbox/`.
2. Never paste passwords, security codes, recovery codes, or secret keys into a chat or vault file.
3. Correct the agent early. You are collaborators; you do not need to accept a direction that feels wrong.

For a fuller explanation, read `SYSTEM-EXPLAINED.md`. After setup, use `setup/POST-SETUP-TUTORIAL.md`.
