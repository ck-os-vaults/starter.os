# decisions

**Bottom line:** Durable owner-confirmed decisions and reasons, newest first. Empty until the owner makes or approves a choice that should guide future work.

**When to read this:** Read when current structure, plans, or boundaries need the reason behind an earlier decision.

## entry format

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — decision

**Decision:** What the owner chose.

**Why:** The reason and important tradeoff.

**Revisit:** Optional condition or date.
```

Do not record exploration, agent opinions, or guesses as decisions.
