# independent review

Use when the owner requests a second opinion or consequential work benefits from genuine dissent.

Give the reviewer the decision, evidence, constraints, current recommendation, and explicit authority boundary. Ask for missed risks, contrary evidence, and specific improvements. Treat review as advice, not proof; verify factual claims directly and let the primary owner make the final decision.
