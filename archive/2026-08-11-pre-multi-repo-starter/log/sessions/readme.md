# sessions

**Bottom line:** Agent handoffs, saved prompts, and session records that preserve continuity when work pauses. They support current status files; they never replace them.

**When to read this:** Read after current status and operating files when resuming a paused task or recovering decisions and watch-outs.

Use `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`. Each handoff states the goal, completed and incomplete work, decisions, important files, validation, watch-outs, and one exact next action.

Superseded or completed handoffs may move to a dated archive after the maintenance safety checks. Verbatim owner transcripts belong in `log/conversations/`.
