# news report

**Bottom line:** Turn owner-selected, verifiable sources into a short cited report that explains what changed, why it matters, and what is worth doing next.

**When to read this:** When the owner asks for a recurring news or update report, chooses sources to monitor, or wants new external capabilities evaluated. Do not use it for general browsing without a defined topic or source boundary.

## procedure

1. Confirm the topics, source list, time window, destination, and authorized access. Prefer primary sources for product changes and label commentary or inference clearly.
2. Check only sources the current environment can access legitimately. Never imply that a private feed, account, or paid service was checked when it was not.
3. Select meaningful items without filling a quota. Deduplicate the same announcement across sources.
4. For each item, provide the source and date, a layman's summary, why it matters to the owner's actual system, and one recommendation: `adopt`, `test`, `watch`, or `ignore`.
5. Put urgent risks or unusually valuable opportunities first. Keep routine noise out.
6. Cite the supporting source near each claim and name any material uncertainty or unavailable source.

## optional scheduled recipe

When the owner accepts a recurring `News Report`, use the verified timezone, owner-chosen schedule, source boundary, and persistent report destination. Update an existing equivalent instead of creating duplicates or a new task for every run.

Setup or update may suggest this recipe only after verifying that useful sources and a suitable research tool are available. No particular news source, social network, search provider, model, or agent is required.

Verify name, schedule, timezone, destination, instructions, source access, runtime, active status, likely cost, and first eligible run.

## boundaries

- Do not publish, post, reply, subscribe, purchase, or change an account.
- Do not treat a model's unsupported summary as a citation.
- Do not copy long source passages or expose private feed contents.
- Do not create a recommendation merely to fill the report.
