# start here — your complete setup

**Audience:** Owner
**Lifecycle:** Setup only — archive with the `setup/` folder after onboarding.

**Bottom line:** Paste the prompt below into Codex or Claude while this folder is open. Your agent will interview you, personalize the context files, protect the system with two private online copies and a daily local backup, test everything, and then prepare your first lesson in working with agents.

**When to read this:** Read this first. You do not need coding knowledge, Git knowledge, or a perfect plan.

## what you need

- This folder open in Codex, Claude Code, or Claude Cowork with permission to read and edit it.
- Obsidian installed or available to install.
- Access to your email and phone or authentication app while securing accounts.
- A password manager or another private place for account recovery codes.
- Ideally, an external drive for the third backup. If you do not have one, the agent will explain the choice and leave a clear next step.

You do **not** need GitHub, GitLab, Git, or backup knowledge. The agent will handle the technical work and explain only what you need to understand or approve.

## what happens next

The agent will guide you through four simple outcomes:

1. **It learns your context.** You talk naturally; it turns the useful parts into organized context files after you approve the summary.
2. **It protects the files.** GitHub and GitLab become two private, matching online histories. Two-factor authentication protects both accounts.
3. **It creates a third backup.** On a Mac, the recommended example is Carbon Copy Cloner running daily to an external drive; an equivalent tool is fine.
4. **It teaches you how to work together.** You receive an interactive beginner tutorial and use a real problem from your life as the first exercise.

After the tutorial, the agent moves the installation and interview documents into a dated archive. Your everyday system stays small, and future chats start with your real context instead of repeating setup.

## one security rule

Passwords, two-factor codes, and recovery codes belong in your password manager—not in this vault and not in an AI chat. Your agent can tell you where to click, then wait while you complete the private step yourself.
